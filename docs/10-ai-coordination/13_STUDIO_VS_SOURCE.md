## Limits on performance and publication claims

- Local installation and `KinBranchRuntimeState=Ready` mean constructors ran. They do *not* mean parcel placement, visual quality, mobile FPS, accessibility or inter-Place travel passed.
- The Code Mode / Python tests are not Roblox Studio engine execution.
- Nine `place_key` declarations do not mean nine real Places have been created or tested in a published Roblox universe.
- A `VideoFrame` cannot directly embed arbitrary YouTube streaming. Treat it as a prominent broadcast-design panel unless a permitted Roblox video is configured.
- The previous visual hotfix may have been applied to the user's local `.rbxl` and may not be present in a new source ZIP; check before claiming it is fixed.
